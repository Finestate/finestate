Privacy

Finestate is a private site used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Where bank accounts are connected, the site reads account balances and transactions on a read-only basis, with the account holder's own consent given at their bank. It cannot make payments or move money.

This data is used only to show the owner their own finances. It is stored securely, is not shared with or sold to anyone, and access can be withdrawn at the bank at any time.
